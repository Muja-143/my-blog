My Story

I am S Arshath Mobika, a Computer Science Engineering graduate who is passionate about technology, teaching and web development.


After completing my B.E Computer Science Engineering, I started my professional journey by working in computer lab management. This experience helped me improve my technical knowledge and problem-solving skills.


Later, I entered the teaching field where I teach Computer Science and Computer Applications. Teaching helped me understand technology from a learner's perspective.


Along with teaching, I started learning website development. I explored WordPress, Wix, ecommerce management and web technologies.


My goal is to become a professional software developer and create useful digital solutions for businesses and educational institutions.
